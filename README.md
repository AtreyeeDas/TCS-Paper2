# Comprehensive Architectural Pivot Report: Zero-Shot Code-Switched Speech Synthesis
## 1. Diagnostic Failure of the Initial Pipeline
Our initial methodology proposed a 5-stage pipeline designed to cure attention collapse in zero-shot code-switched Text-to-Speech (CS-TTS). However, upon physical implementation with the Coqui XTTS-v2 foundation model, the pipeline failed on three critical engineering fronts:
 1. **The Tokenizer Shredding (Stage 1 Failure):** We attempted to feed raw Devanagari (Hindi) alongside Latin (English) text or raw International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) characters into XTTS. XTTS relies on a pre-trained Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) tokenizer optimized for standard Latin text. When encountering Devanagari or raw IPA, the tokenizer shredded the input into unrecognizable sub-word bytes, causing catastrophic mispronunciations and driving our Word Error Rate (WER) above 50%.
 2. **The Training vs. Inference Mismatch (Stage 4 Failure):** Our core novelty was a Phoneme-Aligned Cross-Attention Entropy Loss (\mathcal{L}_{entropy}). However, a loss function is strictly a *training-phase* mathematical construct used for backpropagation. Because we were running inference on a frozen, pre-trained foundation model (XTTS-v2), we could not physically apply this loss. This architectural impossibility led to the calculation of a computationally simulated (fabricated) attention entropy metric, rendering the prior baseline results invalid for academic peer review.
 3. **Flawed Evaluation Metrics:** We utilized Mel-Cepstral Distortion (MCD) to compare our zero-shot cloned audio (using a clean female reference) against the multi-speaker MUCS dataset ground truth. Because MCD measures absolute acoustic distance, the inherent speaker identity mismatch drove the MCD to an artificially distorted ~81 dB. Furthermore, our Levenshtein WER calculations compared Devanagari ground-truth text against Romanized Whisper transcriptions, resulting in false-positive errors due to script mismatch.
## 2. The Two Proposed Alternative Pathways
To salvage the mathematical intent of our research and yield authentic, publishable results, we defined two scientifically grounded pathways:
 * **Approach A (The Training Route):** Retain the exact original 5 stages, including the \mathcal{L}_{entropy} training loss, but discard XTTS-v2. Instead, we would adopt an older, fully trainable architecture like VITS (YourTTS) or Tacotron 2. This would require ~24-36 hours of continuous GPU training on our RTX 5000 Blackwell to prove that our custom loss prevents alignment failure.
 * **Approach B (The Inference Route):** Retain the massive XTTS-v2 foundation model and its zero-shot capabilities, but pivot our core novelty from a *Training-Time Loss* to an *Inference-Time Decoding Intervention*. We would mathematically stabilize the transformer's attention logits during the autoregressive generation loop exactly at the code-switch boundaries.
## 3. Justification for Adopting Approach B
We selected **Approach B** because it is vastly more relevant to the current trajectory of AI research (2025-2027). The speech community has largely moved away from training small models from scratch in favor of utilizing massive, frozen foundation models (VALL-E, XTTS, CosyVoice). Proposing a computationally expensive custom loss function for an older architecture (Approach A) lacks modern scalability.
By contrast, demonstrating that we can cure cross-lingual hallucination (babbling) in a 1-billion-parameter frozen model—purely by intercepting its probability distribution at inference time—represents a highly novel, zero-parameter, computationally cheap solution. This directly addresses the modern problem of foundation model alignment.
## 4. Detailed Implementation of the New Pipeline (Approach B)
Our revised 5-stage architecture specifically targets inference-time generation on autoregressive (AR) models.
### Stage 1: Orthographic Normalization & Boundary Tracking
 * **Implementation:** Instead of mapping to strict IPA, we implement Phonetic Transliteration. We utilize libraries (e.g., ITRANS mapping) to transliterate Devanagari Hinglish into a uniform, BPE-compatible Roman script (e.g., "Hello कैसे हो" \rightarrow "Hello kaise ho"). Crucially, our script tracks the exact token index where the linguistic boundary occurs (Boundary \beta).
 * **Academic Attribution:** *Adapted/Borrowed.* Text normalization and transliteration are standard NLP preprocessing steps. We will explicitly cite foundational transliteration methodologies (e.g., Epitran, standard Indic NLP libraries).
### Stage 2: Speaker Latent Extraction
 * **Implementation:** We extract a 192-dimensional continuous speaker embedding from a 5-second clean reference audio clip.
 * **Academic Attribution:** *Borrowed.* We discard our custom ECAPA-TDNN script and natively use the Perceiver-based speaker encoder built directly into the XTTS-v2 architecture.
### Stage 3: Frozen AR Transformer Backbone
 * **Implementation:** We utilize the XTTS GPT-style autoregressive decoder to generate acoustic semantic tokens. We treat the cross-attention matrix as a frozen black box, relying purely on its output logits.
 * **Academic Attribution:** *Borrowed.* We will cite the VALL-E (Wang et al., 2023) and XTTS (Coqui) papers as our foundational baseline framework.
### Stage 4: Boundary-Aware Inference-Time Intervention (ITI)
 * **Implementation:** This is our primary architectural contribution. We write a PyTorch hook that intercepts the AR generation loop. When the decoder reaches the pre-calculated code-switch token index (\beta), we dynamically apply a Temperature Penalty (T < 1.0) to the output logit tensor before the softmax function. This artificially sharpens the probability distribution, suppressing the localized entropy spike and forcing the model to confidently select the next phoneme and the End-of-Sequence (EOS) token, thereby preventing trailing babble.
 * **Academic Attribution:** *Highly Novel Application.* The mathematical concept of Inference-Time Intervention is *adapted* from Large Language Model (LLM) truthfulness research (e.g., Li et al., *Inference-Time Intervention: Eliciting Truthful Answers*, 2023). However, triggering ITI dynamically based on orthographic boundaries to cure autoregressive acoustic alignment collapse is a **completely novel** contribution to speech synthesis.
### Stage 5: Energy-Gated Acoustic Guardrail
 * **Implementation:** A post-processing DSP script utilizing Librosa to calculate RMS energy and truncate any trailing low-energy acoustic artifacts.
 * **Academic Attribution:** *Adapted.* Voice Activity Detection (VAD) and RMS gating are classic signal processing techniques. Our application of them as an anti-hallucination guardrail is an engineering optimization, holding marginal academic novelty.
## 5. Assessment for ICASSP Acceptance
 * **Impact of the Problem:** High. Cross-lingual hallucination and alignment failure are heavily documented flaws in modern AR speech models.
 * **Feasibility:** High. This pipeline requires zero model training. It relies entirely on Python/PyTorch scripting to manipulate tensors during the forward pass, which our RTX 5000 will execute in seconds.
 * **Effectiveness:** High. By transliterating the text, the model can actually read the Hindi. By applying ITI, we mathematically prevent the model from stuttering at the transition.
 * **Novelty:** Strong. We are bringing cutting-edge LLM activation steering techniques into the audio domain to fix foundation models without the need for expensive fine-tuning. This is a highly attractive narrative for the "Machine Learning for Audio" track.
## 6. Honest Evaluation Metrics
To prove our system works without fabricating attention entropy, we are deploying four rigorous, physically measurable metrics on the MUCS test dataset:
 1. **Strictly Normalized WER (Intelligibility):** We will pass the generated audio through Whisper Large-v3. Before calculating Levenshtein distance, our evaluation script will force both the ground-truth text and the transcription into lowercase, punctuation-free Roman script. *Honest Assessment:* This accurately captures phonetic correctness. We expect the baseline to exhibit high WER (due to token shredding and babbling) and our Full System to drop to 15%-25%.
 2. **SIM-R (Speaker Fidelity):** Cosine similarity between the clean reference voice and the generated audio using ECAPA-TDNN. *Honest Assessment:* Proves our logit intervention does not destroy the cloned voice identity.
 3. **Real-Time Factor (RTF):** Total generation time divided by audio length. *Honest Assessment:* Proves our ITI intervention adds negligible computational overhead compared to the baseline.
 4. **Trailing Hallucination Duration (THD) - (Our Novel Metric):** We subtract the timestamp of the final transcribed word (via Whisper) from the total duration of the .wav file. *Honest Assessment:* This directly replaces the flawed Acoustic Length Ratio (ALR). THD isolates the exact physical footprint of the "babble" independently of how fast the speaker talks. We expect the Baseline to show ~2-5 seconds of THD (hallucinated trailing noise), while our Full System will drive THD down to ~0.1 seconds, mathematically proving that our ITI forces the model to emit the EOS token accurately.
