This is a classic variable name collision. At the top of the script, t_enc is correctly loaded as your Text-NLU label encoders dictionary. However, inside the FIXED WHISPER ENCODER block, the script measures inference latency and assigns it to t_enc = (time.perf_counter() - t0_enc) * 1000.0.
This unintentionally overwrites your dictionary with a float (the time in milliseconds), which causes the pipeline to crash the moment Text-NLU tries to use it.
To fix this, rename the latency variable from t_enc to enc_time. Open run_reasoning_ablation.py and make these two sets of replacements:
1. Update the Fixed Whisper Encoder section (around line 380):
Find the A. FIXED WHISPER ENCODER (CACHED) block and replace the t_enc latency assignments:

            enc_time = (time.perf_counter() - t0_enc) * 1000.0
            print(f"\n[CACHE] Loaded cached Whisper encoder: {sample_id}")
        else:

            with torch.no_grad():

            torch.save(enc_out, cache_file)
            enc_time = (time.perf_counter() - t0_enc) * 1000.0
            print(f"\n[CACHE] Computed and saved Whisper encoder: {sample_id}")

Scroll down to the Condition 1, Condition 2, and Condition 3 blocks and replace t_enc with enc_time in the tot_lat calculations:
